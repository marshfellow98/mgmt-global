## Design tokens

### Color

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#E1A13F` | Gold — primary accent, CTA buttons, eyebrow labels, italic headline phrases |
| `#05070A` | Near-black — deepest background |
| `#10151C` | Charcoal — section backgrounds |
| `#14181D` | Charcoal, one step lighter |
| `#EFCFA0` | Pale gold — hairline rules, light section fills |
| `#FFF9F0` | Warm cream — light backgrounds |
| `#FFFFFF` | White — primary text on dark |

Two globals ship with alpha: body text is `#FFFFFFD1` and secondary is `#05070AD1`, both ~82%. This is why body copy reads grey and slightly muddy on black. **Drop the alpha in the rebuild** and use solid white, or a deliberate warm grey if you want a step down from headings.

### Type

- **Schibsted Grotesk** — everything. Body, headings, labels.
- A serif italic carries the accent phrase in each headline (`Recruiting Firm`, `you are.`, `management solutions.`).
- Roboto and Roboto Slab are set as Elementor globals but never used. Leftover template defaults — ignore.

### Who we are
**Headline:** A Recruiting Firm & a Growth Partner

Most search firms cover every industry and know none of them deeply.

We chose the opposite. MGMTGlobal works only in insurance, companies, wholesale and retail brokerage, captives, and programs, across producer, C-suite, director, and partner roles.

That focus is the whole advantage. We already know the players, the books, and the moves before we start. Our 25-year legacy database and the relationships behind it mean we can identify and approach the right person quickly, quietly, and with the context to know they'll actually fit.

This isn't résumé matching. It's strategic growth, intelligently delivered.

### What we do
**Headline:** End-to-end talent and management solutions.

- **Retained Search** — For the hires that matter most. We take on a limited number of searches at a time so each one gets full attention, and deliver a shortlist of three to four finalists vetted against your culture, goals, and the role itself.
- **Contingent Submittal** (Pay only on placement) — A no-risk way to start. When we already have an exceptional candidate who fits your team, we'll introduce them, and you pay only on a successful placement.
- **M&A Consulting** — Grow by acquisition, not just one hire at a time. Lift-outs, tuck-ins, roll-ins, fold-ins, and acquisitions, we help you bring in entire high-performing teams that are ready to produce on day one.

### How it works — retained search in 4 steps
**Headline:** A search process you can actually understand

1. **We learn your business.** Before we look at a single candidate, we understand your company, your culture, and what success in this role really looks like.
2. **We go to the market, quietly.** We tap our network and 25-year database to find high performers who aren't looking, then approach them with discretion.
3. **We hand you a shortlist, not a pile.** Three or four finalists, fully evaluated on track record, fit, and ambition.
4. **We stay in it to the close.** We help structure interviews, negotiate, and bring the right person across the line.
